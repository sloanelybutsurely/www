Link: [alternativeto.net](https://alternativeto.net/)

I often use AlternativeTo when:

- I like a macOS-only (or Windows-only, or iOS-only, etc.) application and want
  something similar on another platform
- I want an open source alternative to a proprietary piece of software
- I want a self-hosted alternative to a proprietary hosted SaaS product

## Alfred Search

To search AlternativeTo from Alfred add the following custom search:

`https://alternativeto.net/browse/search/?q={query}`

Encode spaces as `+`.
